WhatsApp → Settings → Linked devices → Link a device. This takes one of your four device slots. Your phone keeps working exactly as before.

The archive only ever listens. It sends no messages, marks nothing as read, and stays invisible to the people you're talking to.

Process backlog

Messages arrive on their own while connected — there is nothing to fetch. This works through the ones still waiting to be transliterated and glossed so they become searchable.

AI provider

Most services other than Anthropic speak the OpenAI shape, including local runners. Picking the wrong one gives 404s.

Without a key or a base URL this stays in local-only mode: capture, folding, stemming and keyword search all work and nothing leaves this machine. Otherwise message text is sent to whatever endpoint is configured for glossing and answering — including messages other people wrote. Point the base URL at an Anthropic-compatible service running on this machine and that text never leaves it.
