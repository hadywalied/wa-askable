A local archive of your WhatsApp

It links as a companion device, the same way WhatsApp Web does, and keeps every message it sees in a database on this machine. It never sends anything, and your phone keeps working untouched.

It records from the moment you link it — not before. WhatsApp does not hand over your history. Everything already on your phone stays there; this archive starts empty and fills up from now on. The sooner you link, the more you keep.

Where should the archive live?

One folder holds everything: the database, the media, and your WhatsApp session keys. One thing to back up, encrypt, or delete.

Anyone who can read this folder can impersonate your WhatsApp account. Keep it on an encrypted disk and out of Dropbox, OneDrive or iCloud.

About

The app only captures while it is running. Messages that arrive while it is closed are lost permanently — there is no backfill.
